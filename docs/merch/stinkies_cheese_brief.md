## Nacho Cheese

### Profile
- **Base:** Real cheddar (≥ 40% cheese solids)
- **Heat:** None — pure cheese
- **Texture:** Pourable at room temp, thickens when cold
- **Flavor target:** Sharp cheddar forward, mild salt, slight tang
- **Use:** Chips, fries, hot dogs, anything that needs cheese

### Formula Requirements
- No artificial colors (no FD&C Yellow)
- No HFCS
- Sodium: ≤ 280mg per 2 tbsp serving
- Shelf stable at room temp: ≥ 12 months sealed

---

## Jalapeño Cheese

### Profile
- **Base:** Real cheddar (≥ 40% cheese solids) + diced pickled jalapeño (≥ 8% by weight)
- **Heat:** Medium — 2,500–5,000 SHU range (accessible, not challenge heat)
- **Texture:** Same as nacho — pourable at room temp
- **Flavor target:** Cheese first, jalapeño finish, vinegar brine note from pickle
- **Use:** Everything nacho cheese does + eggs, burritos, nachos with actual heat

### Formula Requirements
- Pickled jalapeños: visible dice (≥ 5mm), not puree
- No artificial colors
- No HFCS
- Sodium: ≤ 310mg per 2 tbsp serving
- Shelf stable at room temp: ≥ 12 months sealed

## QC Protocol

1. Brix/solids: cheese solids ≥ 40% (refractometer check on 3 units per batch)
2. Viscosity: pours within 5 seconds of inversion at 70°F on 5 units
3. Jalapeño dice visibility: ≥ 3 visible pieces per tablespoon (visual, 5 units)
4. Seal integrity: vacuum button depressed on 100% of units before ship
5. Shelf life verification: co-packer provides certificate of analysis per batch
