## Regulatory Reality

Beer sales require:
1. **Retailer liquor license** — state-specific, required to sell alcohol
2. **Three-tier compliance** — manufacturer → distributor → retailer (can't buy direct from Molson Coors)
3. **Age verification** — 21+ required at point of sale
4. **Online shipping** — varies by state; most states prohibit or heavily restrict direct-to-consumer alcohol shipment

**Distribution path:**
- **Physical retail:** Standard retailer license + buy through local Molson Coors distributor
- **Online:** Requires state-by-state licensing; use a licensed alcohol fulfillment partner (Speakeasy Co., Vinoshipper, etc.) for states that permit it
