# STINKIES COMMISSAIRE — Soap

**Status:** LOCKED
**Updated:** 2026-06-27

---

## What It Is

A bar of soap. Lye. No fragrance claim. No moisturizing complex. Soap.

It is free. You take one when you come in. Or we put it in the bag. There is no SKU. There is no subscription.

---

## Spec

- **Type:** Basic lard or tallow lye soap (sodium hydroxide saponification)
- **Size:** 4oz bar — standard hand/body bar
- **Fragrance:** None, or single-note (unscented / plain)
- **Packaging:** None. Unwrapped, or one-color kraft paper band with "STINKIES" stamped on it.
- **Color:** Off-white / cream — whatever the base fats produce naturally

---

## Procurement

Bought in the largest possible quantity. Pallet quantities from a contract soap manufacturer. Target unit cost: **≤ $0.12/bar at pallet MOQ** (2,000–5,000 units).

Emily Prime monitors soap inventory at Store 0 and drafts reorder when below 500 bars. Approval in HEIMDAL. This is the lowest-friction reorder in the entire commissary.

---

## Distribution

- **At Store 0:** Stack of bars on the counter near the register. Take one. No purchase necessary.
- **Online orders:** One bar in every package shipped. Automatically included. No mention on the receipt.
- **No upsell. No "add soap to your cart." It is already in the bag.**
