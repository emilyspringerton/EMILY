# STINKIES COMMISSAIRE — Tortilla Truck

**Status:** CONCEPT LOCKED — site + permits gated (S147-02)
**Updated:** 2026-06-27

---

## What It Is

A truck that makes tortillas. Parked outside Store 0 in Pontiac.

Not a taco truck. Not a food truck selling tacos. A truck that makes fresh tortillas and sells them, warm, by the stack. The tortillas are the product.

---

## The Truck

- **Vehicle:** A converted step van or small box truck — not a glamorous Airstream situation. A working truck.
- **Equipment onboard:**
  - Commercial tortilla press (electric, tabletop, ~300 tortillas/hr capacity)
  - Two-burner flat griddle (propane)
  - Masa bin (50lb+ capacity, fresh masa or maseca depending on sourcing)
  - Warming drawer for finished stacks
  - Small water tank + handwashing sink (health code)
  - A sign: TORTILLAS — STINKIES COMMISSAIRE
- **Position:** Curbside or parking lot, directly adjacent to Store 0. The truck is visible from the store window.

**Masa spec:** Traditional corn masa — white or yellow corn, cal (slaked lime), water. No flour tortillas from this truck. Corn only.

**Nothing else.** The truck makes tortillas. That's it. You can put the nacho cheese on them if you go inside and get the cheese. You can put a hot dog in one if you want. The truck makes tortillas.

## Regulatory (Michigan Mobile Food Unit)

- **Michigan Mobile Food Service License** (MDHHS) — required for food prep on a truck
- **City of Pontiac mobile vending permit** — site-specific
- **Fire safety inspection** (propane equipment)
- **Commissary agreement** — Michigan requires mobile food units to have a licensed commissary kitchen for cleaning/storage. Store 0 itself may qualify once the kitchen is built, or a commercial kitchen agreement is needed.

Emily Prime tracks license renewals. All permits executed by Emily (human).
