## SC-JAL-02 — Jalapeño & Cheddar Stuffed Hot Dogs

**What it is:** 100% beef hot dog, natural casing, with a jalapeño and cheddar filling core. The standard STINKIES hot dog is the base. This version has the filling.

- **Fill:** Pickled jalapeño dice + cheddar blend piped into center during production
- **Heat level:** 3,500–6,000 SHU (hotter than the cheese sauce — the dog has to be worth ordering over the regular)
- **Casing:** Natural (hog)
- **Size:** 2oz per link, 8 links / 16oz pack
- **Format:** Frozen, ships with dry ice
- **Packaging:** Same matte black vacuum-seal as standard hot dog, black label with "JALAPEÑO & CHEDDAR" in white
- **Price:** $14 / 8-pack
- **Unit cost target:** ≤ $5.50 at 200-pack MOQ (higher than standard due to filling)
- **Margin:** ≥ 61%

**STINKIES JALAPEÑO KIT:** Jalapeño dogs + jalapeño cheese + jalapeño pickles = $26 (vs $28 separate). The jalapeño version of the Nacho Kit.

## SC-JAL-03 — Jalapeño Pickles

**What it is:** Whole cucumber pickles, aggressively spiced with fresh jalapeño, garlic, dill.

- **Style:** Whole spears or halves — not chips, not slices
- **Brine:** Vinegar-based, dill, garlic, fresh jalapeño slices visible in the jar
- **Heat level:** 4,000–7,000 SHU — these are not decorative
- **Jar:** 24oz glass, matte black label, STINKIES COMMISSAIRE, white ink
- **Price:** $6 / 24oz jar
- **Unit cost target:** ≤ $1.80 at 500-jar MOQ
- **Margin:** ≥ 70%
- **Subscription:** $10/mo 2-jar sub

## SC-JAL-04 — Jalapeño Tortilla Chips

**What it is:** Thick-cut corn tortilla chips with jalapeño seasoning. Made for scooping the cheese sauce.

- **Cut:** Restaurant-style thick chip — not thin, not the grocery store mass-market chip. Structural enough to hold a full scoop of hot cheese sauce.
- **Seasoning:** Jalapeño lime — coarse salt, jalapeño powder, citric acid
- **Corn:** White corn (consistent with the tortilla truck masa)
- **Bag:** 9oz matte black bag, "STINKIES JALAPEÑO CHIPS" white block caps
- **Price:** $4 / 9oz bag
- **Unit cost target:** ≤ $1.20 at 500-bag MOQ
- **Margin:** ≥ 70%

**THE PLAY:** Jalapeño chips + jalapeño cheese sauce = $12 combination at Store 0. Customers figure this out on their own. We do not need to bundle it formally.
