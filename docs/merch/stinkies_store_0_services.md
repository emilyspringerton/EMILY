## Public Bathrooms

Store 0 has a public restroom. Open to anyone. No purchase required.

This is not negotiable. A commissary without a bathroom is not a commissary. The bathroom is clean. It is stocked. It always has soap (STINKIES bars, obviously). It always has paper. The Counter Person checks it every 2 hours.

**The economics of a clean public restroom in Pontiac:**
- People who use the bathroom come back
- People who need a bathroom remember where one was
- The alternative (no public restroom) communicates: this is not your place

Store 0 is your place. The bathroom is open.

**Spec:**
- 1 single-occupancy unisex restroom
- ADA compliant
- Automatic soap dispenser (STINKIES bulk liquid reformulation — same lye base, pump format for wall mount)
- Paper towels (not air dryer — air dryers are not what a commissary does)
- Checked every 2 hours, logged by Counter Person
- Emily Prime does not monitor the bathroom. The Counter Person does. This is as it should be.

## Michigan Lottery

Store 0 is a Michigan Lottery retailer.

**What we sell:**
- Instant tickets (scratch-offs) — all currently available denominations
- Lotto 47, Classic Lotto 47, Keno, Daily 3, Daily 4, Fantasy 5
- No online/digital lottery — physical tickets only

**Why:**
A commissary sells lottery. This is non-negotiable. People buy cigarettes and a scratch-off. People buy coffee and a ticket. The lottery is not a bet that we endorse as smart — it is an acknowledgment that our customers make their own decisions and we carry what they want.

**License:** Michigan Lottery Retailer License — applied through Michigan Lottery Retailer Services. Emily Prime tracks the application deadline and renewal dates, files Apple reminders 60 days before renewal.

**Revenue:** Michigan Lottery pays retailers a commission on ticket sales (typically 5–6% of ticket face value + bonus commissions on winners sold). Low margin but zero cost of goods.

**Emily Prime role:** None in daily operation. Emily Prime monitors license renewal dates only.
