## Regulatory Reality

Cigarettes in the US require:
1. **TTB permit** (Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau) — required to manufacture or import
2. **FDA registration** as a tobacco product manufacturer or importer
3. **State tobacco licenses** — varies by state, required for each state sold in
4. **MSA participation** or bond — required in most states for new manufacturers
5. **Health warning compliance** — FDA Deeming Rule mandates rotating warnings

**Path:** Private-label through an existing FDA-registered manufacturer. STINKIES is the brand; the manufacturer handles compliance. This is the same model used by store-brand cigarettes.

**Sales channel:** Online tobacco sales require age verification (PACT Act compliance) — no standard WooCommerce checkout. Requires dedicated age-gated flow + carrier compliance (FedEx/UPS no longer ship cigarettes to consumers; USPS banned; requires state-licensed carrier or direct pickup / third-party tobacco retailer distribution).

**Online direct-to-consumer cigarette shipping is not viable under current US law.** Distribution must be through licensed tobacco retailers or direct pickup.
