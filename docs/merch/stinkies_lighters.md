## SC-LIT-01 — STINKIES Lighter

A BIC-style disposable lighter with STINKIES custom print. Matte black body. "STINKIES" in white block caps on the face. That's it.

- **Mechanism:** Standard flint wheel, disposable butane
- **Body:** Full-size (not mini), matte black
- **Print:** "STINKIES" white block caps, centered on face — one color, one word
- **Source:** Custom-printed BIC or BIC equivalent — minimum 144-unit print run (standard promotional lighter MOQ)
- **Price:** $3
- **Unit cost target:** ≤ $0.80 at 500-unit MOQ (custom print)
- **Margin:** ≥ 73%

Lives at the register. Impulse buy. The cigarette customer buys a pack and a lighter. The commissary staple.

## SC-LIT-02 — EINHORN R&D Lighter

Not a disposable. A custom-designed refillable lighter with the EINHORN_INDUSTRIAL mark. This is the R&D division's physical artifact — one of the few EINHORN_INDUSTRIAL branded objects that exists in the physical world.

- **Mechanism:** Refillable butane, flint wheel or piezo (TBD on prototype)
- **Body:** Heavier gauge steel or aluminum — the weight is the point. You notice it in your hand.
- **Finish:** Matte black, laser-etched
- **Face:** EINHORN antler mark (the R&D symbol) — no text, no wordmark, just the mark. Clean.
- **Back:** "EINHORN" in small block caps, bottom edge. Subtle.
- **Included:** Butane refill adapter card in the box (instructions + adapter). The lighter is refillable — this is explicitly not a disposable.
- **Packaging:** Small matte black box. The EINHORN mark debossed on the lid. No excessive packaging. The lighter is the object.
- **Price:** $18
- **Unit cost target:** ≤ $5 at 200-unit MOQ
- **Margin:** ≥ 72%

Available at Store 0 counter, behind glass or on display near ULTRA toothbrushes. Not an impulse buy — someone picks it up, reads the mark, decides.
