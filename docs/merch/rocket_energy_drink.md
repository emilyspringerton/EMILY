## The Formula (Proprietary)

ROCKET is not a commodity energy drink with a new label. The formula is differentiated.

**Active stack:**
- **Caffeine:** 150mg per 12oz can (not the 300mg race-to-the-bottom — sustainable, functional)
- **Lion's Mane mushroom extract:** 500mg per can (Hericium erinaceus, standardized to 30% beta-glucans) — cognitive clarity, nerve growth factor support. This is the differentiator. Most energy drinks do not have lion's mane at this dose.
- **L-Theanine:** 100mg — pairs with caffeine to smooth the edge, extend the curve
- **B-complex:** B6 (2mg), B12 (500mcg), Niacin (20mg) — standard energy stack
- **Taurine:** 1,000mg
- **Carbonated water, citric acid, natural flavors**

No proprietary blends that obscure dosing. Every active ingredient labeled with its exact amount.

**Flavor:** One flavor. ROCKET ORIGINAL — citrus forward (lime + grapefruit), clean finish, no cloying sweetness. Sweetened with a blend of cane sugar (minimal) and stevia. Not sugar-free, not a sugar bomb.

**What ROCKET is not:** No artificial colors. No "energy blend" hiding undisclosed stimulants. No mystery.
