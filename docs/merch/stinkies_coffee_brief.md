## What STINKIES Coffee Is

STINKIES COMMISSAIRE is a commissary. It sells what you need. Coffee is what you need. Two blends — one for working, one for drinking. No origin story. No single-origin Guatemala micro-lot. Coffee.

## Mountain Man

### Profile
- **Roast:** Dark (Vienna–French border)
- **Flavor target:** Smoke, dark chocolate, low acid, long finish
- **Body:** Heavy
- **Caffeine:** High
- **Intended brew method:** French press, cowboy pot, percolator — anything coarse and direct

### Sourcing
- **Origin:** Blend — Brazil (60%) + Sumatra (40%)
- **Certifications:** None required. Rainforest Alliance if vendor offers at no premium.
- **Processing:** Natural/dry preferred for body

### Packaging
- **Bag:** Matte kraft, resealable, 12oz
- **Color:** Black ink on kraft
- **Label:** "MOUNTAIN MAN" in block caps. "STINKIES COMMISSAIRE" below in small caps. Roast level. Weight. Nothing else.
- **Valve:** One-way degassing valve required (freshness)

## Powderhorn

### Profile
- **Roast:** Medium-light (City+)
- **Flavor target:** Citrus, honey, clean, mild acid, short finish
- **Body:** Medium
- **Caffeine:** Standard
- **Intended brew method:** Pour-over, drip, Aeropress — anything that rewards clarity

### Sourcing
- **Origin:** Colombian single-origin (100%) — Huila or Nariño preferred
- **Certifications:** None required. Colombian export certification standard.
- **Processing:** Washed preferred for clarity

### Packaging
- **Bag:** Matte kraft, resealable, 12oz
- **Color:** White ink on kraft
- **Label:** "POWDERHORN" in block caps. "STINKIES COMMISSAIRE" below in small caps. Roast level. Weight. Nothing else.
- **Valve:** One-way degassing valve required

## QC Protocol

1. Cupping: both blends cupped against target profile before batch approval
2. Roast date stamp: verify ≤ 7 days on 5 units per batch
3. Seal integrity: squeeze test — no air infiltration on 10 units per batch
4. Weight: ≥ 340g net on 5 units per batch

## Powderhorn Instant Espresso

Two formats. Same Colombian single-origin base as the whole bean. Freeze-dried.

### Jar (Multi-serve)

- **Contents:** 3.5oz (100g) freeze-dried instant espresso — approx. 50 shots
- **Packaging:** Matte black glass jar, metal lid, "POWDERHORN INSTANT ESPRESSO" label (white ink)
- **Price:** $22
- **Unit cost target:** ≤ $7.00 at 150-jar MOQ
- **Gross margin:** ≥ 68%
- **Use case:** Desk, travel kit, anywhere you can't brew

### Singles (STINKIES-branded, $1) — Loss Leader

- **Contents:** 2g single-serve instant espresso sachet — one double shot
- **Packaging:** Matte black foil sachet, 65mm × 90mm
- **Brand:** **STINKIES COMMISSAIRE** (not Powderhorn) — impulse, trial, entry
- **Price:** $1.00 per single / $10 for 12-pack
- **Unit cost target:** ~$0.80–$1.00 per sachet at MOQ — intentionally at or below break-even
- **Gross margin:** Negative to zero. **This is a loss leader.**
- **Use case:** Point-of-sale add-on, first-time trial, corner store, event handout
- **Conversion target:** ≥ 15% of single buyers purchase a jar within 30 days

The $1 single is not a product. It is advertising with a price tag. The unit economics are the jar ($22) and the subscription ($29/mo). Every dollar spent on singles is a customer acquisition cost, not a margin event. Track it as CAC, not revenue.
