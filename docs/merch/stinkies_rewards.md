# STINKIES REWARDS — Punch Card
## No App. No Data. No Registration.

**Status:** LOCKED — print run gated (S157-02)
**Updated:** 2026-06-28

---

## What It Is

A paper punch card. Buy 10 items, get 1 free.

That's it. No app. No loyalty account. No email capture. No points system. No expiration date. A card with 10 boxes and a hole punch behind the counter.

---

## The Card

- **Size:** Business card (3.5" × 2")
- **Front:** Matte black. "STINKIES COMMISSAIRE" in white block caps. 10 blank boxes in a row.
- **Back:** "Buy 10, get 1 free. No expiration. No bullshit." — that's the full terms and conditions.
- **Printing:** Offset or digital, heavyweight card stock. Same matte black as everything else.
- **Cost:** ~$0.03/card at 5,000-card run

---

## How It Works

You buy something. Counter Person punches a box. Buy 10 things, all 10 boxes punched. You get the 11th item free — whatever you want, up to $8 value.

- Punch is per transaction, not per item
- Every purchase counts: coffee, tortilla, taco, ROCKET, beer, hot dog, cheese, TP, soap, lighter
- Free item cap: $8 (covers everything except the hoodie, the EINHORN lighter, and ULTRA toothbrush)
- Lost cards are lost. We don't track them. We don't replace them. The card is yours.

---

## What The Free Item Can Be

Anything priced ≤ $8:
- Any food item (taco, cheese, hot dogs, jalapeño pickles, chips)
- ROCKET energy drink ($4)
- Scott TP ($1.50)
- Soap (already free, but if you want it on the card, sure)
- Coffee (any)
- Lighter (STINKIES $3)
- Lottery scratch-off ($1–$5)

Cannot be redeemed for: EINHORN R&D lighter, ULTRA toothbrush, hoodie, trucker hat, alcohol (liquor license terms).

---

## Economics

Average basket: $8. 10 punches = $80 spent. Free item value: $4–$8 average.

Effective discount: 5–10%. Gross margin across the basket is 65–80%. After punch card redemption: still 55–75% margin on the relationship.

This is not a discount program. This is a reason to come back.

---

## Card Distribution

- One card per customer at first purchase — Counter Person offers it, doesn't push it
- Stack on the counter next to the soap
- At CARTEL bar: cards available at the bar, punched for each drink purchase
- Not printed on the receipt. Not digital. Not in an app. A card.

Emily Prime tracks nothing about this program except the reorder threshold for card stock (reorder at 500 cards remaining).

---

## Why No App

An app captures data and creates friction. A punch card creates a habit.

The person who carries a STINKIES punch card in their wallet is a customer. The person who downloaded a loyalty app that requires a login is a data point.

We want customers.
