## What It Is

The same truck. The same press. The same griddle. The same fresh corn tortillas.

In the morning, the tortilla truck is a breakfast taco truck. 7 AM to 11 AM, it makes breakfast tacos. 11 AM onward, it shifts to plain tortilla retail and B2B wholesale. One truck, two modes, all day.

## Operations

- **Hours:** 7:00 AM – 11:00 AM (breakfast mode) → 11:00 AM – close (tortilla mode)

## The Truck-to-Truck Morning Angle

Other breakfast trucks and taco trucks operating morning markets (Eastern Market, Pontiac weekend markets) buy their tortillas from distributors. The STINKIES truck is already making them fresh at 6:30 AM.

Morning wholesale window: **6:00 AM – 7:00 AM** — other food trucks can pick up fresh morning tortillas before their own service starts. Cash or invoice. No minimum. Bring your own bag.

## Why This Works

The commissary opens at 7 AM. Store 0 has coffee ready. The truck has breakfast tacos ready. ROCKET is in the cooler.

That is a complete morning for a person going to work.

The tortilla truck running breakfast tacos is not a new concept. It is the correct use of an asset that is already there, already making tortillas, already running the press at 6:30 AM.
