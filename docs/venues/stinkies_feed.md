# THE FEED
## STINKIES COMMISSAIRE — Store 0, Pontiac, Michigan

**Status:** OPERATIONAL on day one of Store 0 open
**Updated:** 2026-06-27

---

## What It Is

Every day Store 0 is open, anyone who is hungry gets a free meal. No questions. No ID. No registration. No means test. You say you need a meal, you get one.

This is not a program. This is not a partnership with a nonprofit. This is not a press release. This is a store that feeds people.

---

## The Meal

One hot dog (all-beef, natural casing) in two fresh corn tortillas from the truck, with nacho cheese sauce. One cup of Mountain Man coffee (or water, or both).

That's the meal. It's real food. It's hot. It takes 90 seconds to make.

---

## How It Works

Counter person asks: "What can I get you?"

If you say "I need a meal" or "I'm hungry" or nothing at all — if you come in and it is clear that you need food — you get the meal.

No ticket system. No voucher. No waiting line separate from the regular line. You wait in the same line as everyone else and you get served the same way.

The Counter Person rings it as FEED in the POS. No charge. Emily Prime tracks the count daily.

---

## Volume + Budget

| | |
|---|---|
| Meals/day target | 30–50 |
| Meal cost (all-in, at cost) | ~$0.95 |
| Daily cost | ~$30–$50 |
| Monthly cost | ~$900–$1,500 |
| Annual cost | ~$10,800–$18,000 |

$18,000/year maximum. This is not a line item worth deliberating over. It is the most important $18,000 in the commissary budget.

Emily Prime monitors the daily FEED count and adjusts hot dog + tortilla + cheese reorder thresholds to include FEED volume. The inventory is shared — there are no FEED-specific reserves. The meal comes from the same stock as everything else.

---

## What We Don't Do

- We don't ask why you need it
- We don't make you prove anything
- We don't track who comes in
- We don't announce this program publicly or put a sign in the window about it
- We don't call it charity

---

## Why

Pontiac has people who are hungry. We have food. The math is not complicated.

The soap is free. The bathroom is open. The meal is free if you need it. That's what a commissary is.
