## What CARTEL Is

A nightclub in Pontiac, Michigan. No theme. No gimmick. No concept-forward branding on the walls. A room with a sound system and a bar and a door. The name is CARTEL. That's the concept.

CARTEL is the nightlife arm of EINHORN_INDUSTRIAL. Store 0 is the commissary. CARTEL is what happens after.

---

## The Room

- **Format:** Warehouse/industrial — poured concrete floors, exposed ceiling, no decorative effort beyond lighting rig and sound system
- **Capacity target:** 250–400 occupancy
- **Layout:**
  - Main floor: DJ booth elevated rear-center, dance floor forward
  - Bar: full-length left wall, 8–10 seats
  - Back room: 40-person capacity, lower volume, for people who came together but need to talk
  - Coat check: mandatory on cold nights, staffed
  - No VIP. No bottle service section. One room (plus the back room). You are at CARTEL.
- **Sound system:** This is not negotiable and is the first capital expenditure after lease signing. Professional touring-grade system. Subwoofers in the floor. The sound is the reason to come.
- **Lighting:** Minimal. Not a laser show. Not a light wall. Correct lighting for the room — functional strobes, a few moving heads. The music is the experience, not the lights.

## The Bar

CARTEL has a full on-premises liquor license. The bar is the revenue engine.

**What CARTEL pours:**
- Miller High Life (can — $5 at the bar, same product, same source as Store 0)
- Well liquor (standard bar kit: vodka, gin, rum, tequila, whiskey)
- No craft cocktail menu. No seasonal specials. A short drink list executed correctly.
- **CARTEL Coffee:** Mountain Man dark roast, cold-brewed, served over ice or blended with well whiskey. One drink. Made correctly. $9.
- Shots: $6 standard. $5 Miller High Life cans.
- Water: free. Always. No exceptions.

**What CARTEL does not pour:**
- Novelty shots
- Anything that requires more than 30 seconds to make at volume
- Anything that requires a special garnish program

**Cigarettes:** Sold at the bar. $14/pack — same as Store 0. Michigan has an indoor smoking ban; CARTEL complies. Smokers go outside. CARTEL has a covered outdoor area with heaters for this.

**STINKIES COMMISSAIRE products visible at CARTEL:**
- Miller High Life branded in the bar (cans, always cold)
- Mountain Man coffee behind the bar (CARTEL Coffee cocktail)
- STINKIES merchandise rack near coat check (hoodies, trucker hats)
- Soap in the bathrooms (lye bars, same pallet stock)

## The Door

Cover charge: $10–$20 depending on the night and who is playing.

No guestlist theater. No "I know the promoter" back-channeling that creates a two-tier experience. If you know the promoter, you still pay. The money goes to the artist. This is known.

ID check. 21+ always. No exceptions.
