# Inbox — no-context dump

*Founder-facing scratch space for anything shared without a clear task attached — a link, a
stray idea, "hold this thought." The bar for entry here is zero: nothing needs to be understood,
scoped, or actionable before it lands. What's mandatory is that it lands somewhere, timestamped,
with the full raw content, rather than evaporating at the end of a conversation. See
`EMILY/docs/THE_EMILY_WAY.md` Principle 16.*

*Not the same thing as `BACKLOG.md`'s `INTAKE QUEUE` — that's for observations already curated
toward becoming real backlog items. This is upstream of that: things that haven't been triaged at
all yet. When an entry's intent becomes clear, promote it to `BACKLOG.md` and flip its status to
`triaged` here (don't delete it). Newest dated section on top, same convention as every
`CHANGELOG.md`.*
